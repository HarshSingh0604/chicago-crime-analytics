Static showcase of the Use Case 3 outputs.

Crime Intensity by Hour

Key Insights

  • Crime activity remains relatively low during the early morning hours, with the lowest observed frequency occurring around 05:00.
  • Recorded crime frequency begins to increase substantially from the morning onward, with a clear rise through the afternoon hours.
  • The highest observed crime activity occurs around 18:00, followed by similarly high levels at 19:00 and 20:00.
  • After the evening peak, crime frequency declines steadily, with a particularly noticeable reduction from 21:00 through 23:00.
  • Overall, the distribution shows a clear concentration of recorded crime activity during the afternoon and evening hours compared with the early morning.

Community Area Crime Distribution

Key Insights

  • The distribution has a mean crime count of 25.97 per community area, with the middle 50% of areas ranging from 22 to 29 incidents.
  • The interquartile range (IQR) is 7, giving an IQR-based lower bound of 11.5 and an upper bound of 39.5 incidents.
  • Community Area 56 is the only community area identified as an extreme outlier, with 76 recorded crimes — substantially above the upper IQR bound of 39.5.
  • This means that only 1 community area falls outside the expected IQR range, while the remaining areas lie within the typical distribution.
  • Overall, the box plot and IQR analysis indicate a right-skewed distribution, driven primarily by the unusually high crime count in Community Area 56.

Correlation Matrix

Key Insights

  • The correlation matrix shows that most selected variables have very weak relationships, with correlation values generally close to zero.
  • The strongest observed relationship with arrest is with domestic, with a correlation of 0.07. This indicates only a very weak positive association.
  • The strongest negative relationship shown is between domestic and community_code, at approximately -0.07, which is also very weak.
  • Variables such as year, month, hour, ward_no, beat_num, and district_code show correlations close to zero with arrest, suggesting little linear association in this dataset.
  • Overall, the matrix indicates no strong linear relationships among the selected crime-related features. These correlations describe association only and should not be interpreted as evidence of causation.
