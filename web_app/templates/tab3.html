Crime Trend Over Years

Key Insights

  • Crime counts fluctuate considerably across the observed years rather than following a consistent upward or downward trend.
  • 2016 records the highest crime count in the dataset, while 2017 shows the lowest.
  • Crime levels rise again after the sharp decline in 2017, reaching another high point in 2019.
  • From 2019 onward, crime counts generally decline, with a brief increase in 2021.
  • The overall pattern indicates significant year-to-year variation rather than sustained long-term growth or fall.

Top 10 Crime Categories

Key Insights

  • THEFT is the most frequently recorded crime category, with a clear lead over the other categories.
  • BATTERY ranks second, followed by ASSAULT, showing a noticeable difference between the leading categories and the rest.
  • NARCOTICS, BURGLARY, and ROBBERY form the next major group of frequently recorded crime categories.
  • MOTOR VEHICLE THEFT and VANDALISM occur at lower frequencies than the leading categories.
  • PUBLIC PEACE VIOLATION and DECEPTIVE PRACTICE have the lowest counts among the ten categories shown.
  • Overall, the distribution is concentrated in a small number of crime categories, with THEFT accounting for the largest share of the top-10 group.

Arrest Rate by Year

Key Insights

  • The arrest rate varies across the observed years, indicating that the proportion of reported incidents resulting in an arrest was not constant.
  • The arrest rate reaches its highest level in 2018, at approximately 34%.
  • 2016 records the lowest arrest rate, at approximately 26%.
  • After increasing from 2016 to 2018, the arrest rate generally declines through 2021 before rising again in 2022.
  • The rate falls again in 2023, returning to approximately the same level observed in 2015.
  • Overall, the chart shows noticeable year-to-year variation in arrest outcomes rather than a consistent long-term trend.

Crime Frequency: Month vs Day of Week

Key Insights

  • November on Thursday records the highest observed crime frequency at 34 incidents, making it the most prominent month-and-day combination in the heatmap.
  • Other relatively high-frequency combinations include October and November on Monday, July on Sunday, and December on Saturday.
  • The lowest observed frequency is 15 incidents, occurring on April on Monday and May on Saturday.
  • Crime frequency shows noticeable variation across both months and weekdays, with no single day consistently recording the highest activity across all months.
  • Overall, the heatmap indicates that crime activity is distributed unevenly across the calendar, with certain month-and-weekday combinations showing distinctly higher concentrations than others.

Top Community Areas

Key Insights

  • Community Area 31 ranks second with 38 incidents, while Community Area 55 follows with 35 incidents.
  • Community Areas 70 and 49 each record 33 incidents, placing them just below the leading three areas.
  • The remaining six areas — 21, 36, 44, 50, and 32 — are closely grouped at around 32 incidents, indicating relatively similar crime counts among them.
  • The large gap between Area 56 and the rest of the top 10 indicates that crime records are strongly concentrated in a small number of community areas.
